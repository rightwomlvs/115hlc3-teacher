Stage 1 ｜ 概念啟航
技術高中英文 (三) Lesson 2: Colors Speak Louder than Words
雙模式課堂教學互動講義 ｜ 第一關：色彩心理學概念地圖與行銷導讀
💡 核心概念地圖（Concept Map） 掌握色彩行銷三大關鍵
🌡️ 1. 色溫分類（Color Temperature）
• Warm Colors（暖色系）：紅、橙、黃。
• Cool Colors（冷色系）：藍、綠、灰。
• 品牌利用顏色影響顧客情緒與購買決定。
🔥 2. 暖色給人的感受（Warm Colors）
• 傳達 fun（樂趣）& energy（活力）、appetite（食慾）。
• 代表品牌：McDonald's、Post-it（黃色商標、色彩主視覺）。
💧 3. 冷色給人的感受（Cool Colors）
• 傳達 peacefulness（平靜）、trust（信任）、professional（專業）。
• 適用場所：hospitals（醫院）、offices（辦公室）。
👀 4. 90 秒決定第一印象（90-Second Judgment）
• 消費者常在看到產品後 90 秒內做出潛意識判斷（subconscious judgment）。
• 顏色就是品牌的第一句話——Colors speak louder than words.
📝 破冰檢核：色彩行銷是非題（True or False） 觀念釐清
( O ) 1. Warm colors like red and yellow can make people feel energetic and excited.
( X ) 2. Hospitals usually choose warm colors because they are exciting and lively.
( O ) 3. Consumers may make a subconscious judgment about a product within 90 seconds after they see it.
📖 Reading Tips（閱讀技巧）— 因果關係信號詞（Signal Words for Cause and Effect）
• Cause（原因）：because / since / as（因為）
• Effect（結果）：so / so that / that's why（所以……，因此……）

🔍 任務二：文法焦點——分詞構句（二選一圈選） 簡化對等子句
💡 語法鷹架公式：S ＋ V ..., V-ing / Vpp ...（兩位「作者相同」的連續動作，省略 and＋主詞，動詞化為分詞）
• 主動動作 ➔ V-ing（現在分詞）｜ 被動動作 ➔ Vpp（過去分詞）
1. Toby looked at the color chart, [ taking / taken ] notes for the design.
2. The old piano stands in the corner, [ covering / covered ] in thick dust.
3. The fans rushed to the stage, [ cheering / cheered ] loudly for their idol.
4. Lisa left the party early, [ not noticing / not noticed ] by anyone. *(否定＋被動)*

Stage 4 ｜ 句型特訓
技術高中英文 (三) Lesson 2: Colors Speak Louder than Words
第四關：文法焦點強化 ＆ 句型改寫演練
📝 任務一：文法焦點一——分詞構句（Participle Clauses） 簡化對等子句
💡 簡化三步驟：① 省略連接詞 and ② 省略第二句主詞 ③ 動詞改分詞（主動→V-ing，被動→Vpp；否定在分詞前加 not）
例：He unlocked the door, and he entered the house. ＝ He unlocked the door, entering the house.
1. The teacher walked into the classroom, and she carried a big box of posters.
＝ The teacher walked into the classroom, carrying a big box of posters.
2. The pizza was left on the table, and it was covered with cheese and sauce.
＝ The pizza was left on the table, covered with cheese and sauce.
3. He just stayed at home, and he did not go anywhere.
＝ He just stayed at home, not going anywhere.
✏️ 任務二：文法焦點二——It is said / believed / reported that ... 虛主詞句型
💡 句型公式：It is said / believed / reported（據說∕據信∕據報導）that ＋ S ＋ V
• 虛主詞 it 取代真正的主詞（that 子句），避免主詞過長。
• 時態：現在式（陳述事實）/ 過去式（過去事件）/ will（將來的預測）。
( B )
1. ______ that yellow is the color that best grabs people's attention.
(A) It says (B) It is said (C) Some say (D) People are said
( A )
2. It is believed ______ AI will change the future of education.
(A) that (B) what (C) which (D) where
( C )
3. It is ______ that the new shopping mall will open next month.
(A) say (B) said to (C) reported (D) reporting
4. believed / It is / in the future / that / most cars will be electric（句子重組）
＝ It is believed that most cars will be electric in the future.
