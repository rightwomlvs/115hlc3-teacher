雙模式課堂教學互動講義 ｜ 第一關：避難包概念地圖與防災導讀
💡 核心概念地圖（Concept Map） 掌握三大黃金關鍵
🎒 1. 什麼是 B.O.B.？（Definition）
• B.O.B. 代表 "Bug-Out Bag"（緊急避難包）。
• 設計初衷：面臨大地震或突發災難時，能在 30 秒內隨手抓取逃生，維持 72 小時生存所需。
🏃 2. 雙肩背包 vs. 行李箱（Why Backpack?）
• 首選雙肩背包（Backpack）而非拉桿箱（Suitcase）。
• 核心關鍵：Leaves your hands free（騰出雙手），便於爬梯、攀扶障礙物或協助家人。
📦 3. 防災核心物資配置（Supplies）
• Core Essentials（必備）：飲用水、耐放乾糧罐頭、手電筒、急救箱、身分證件影本。
• Optional Items（選配）：備用鑰匙、應急現金、輕便雨衣、個人藥品、寵物口糧。
💬 4. 防災實用經典諺語（Proverbs）
• "Save some for a rainy day."（未雨綢繆，平時儲備應急物資）。
• "Better safe than sorry."（預防勝於治療；平時做好萬全準備，勝過事後懊悔）。
📝 破冰檢核：避難概念是非題（True or False） 觀念釐清
( O ) 1. B.O.B. stands for "bug-out bag," which is a survival kit designed for emergencies.
( X ) 2. A rolling suitcase is better than a backpack because it allows you to pack more luxury items.
( X ) 3. Once you pack your B.O.B., you should leave it untouched in the closet for ten years.
💡 防災生活素養小叮嚀：定期檢查是維護避難包的關鍵！專家建議每六個月（6 months）檢查更新一次食品有效期限、電池電力與個人常備藥品。

✏️ 任務二：句型合併與重組（Sentence Construction） 附 3～4 行手寫留白草稿框
1. Jay finished his homework early. / Jay helped other students with theirs.（用 not only … but also 合併）
＝ Jay not only finished his homework early but also helped other students with theirs.
2. The shaking stopped. / Jasmine grabbed her B.O.B. and headed for the door.（用 As soon as … 合併）
＝ As soon as the shaking stopped, Jasmine grabbed her B.O.B. and headed for the door.
3. can / keep / A balanced diet / your mind / sharp（句子重組）
＝ A balanced diet can keep your mind sharp.
4. running / when you / Do not / brush your teeth / leave the water（句子重組）
＝ Do not leave the water running when you brush your teeth.
