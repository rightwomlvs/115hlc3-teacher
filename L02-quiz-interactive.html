✏️ 三、句型練習（每題 8 分，共 40 分） 分詞構句｜in order to｜句子重組
1. The school's new library is bright and modern, and it provides students with a comfortable place to study.（用分詞構句改寫畫底線部分）
providing students with a comfortable place to study.
2. Kelly arrived fifteen minutes early. ＋ Kelly wasn't late for her interview.（用 in order not to 合併）
Kelly arrived fifteen minutes early in order not to be late for her interview.
3. not feeling / with confidence, / The actor / nervous at all / came on the stage（句子重組）
The actor came on the stage with confidence, not feeling nervous at all.
4. are closed / because of / that two roads / It is reported / the strong typhoon（句子重組）
It is reported that two roads are closed because of the strong typhoon.
5. her friend's / smiled at the card, / warm words / Layla / touched by（句子重組）
Layla smiled at the card, touched by her friend's warm words.
