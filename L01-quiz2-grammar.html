三、句型練習（每題 8 分，共 40 分）
1. Jay finished his homework early. / Jay helped other students with theirs.（用 not only … but also 合併句子）
＝ Jay not only finished his homework early but also helped other students with theirs.
2. The bell rang. / All the students rushed out of the classroom.（用 As soon as … 合併句子）
＝ As soon as the bell rang, all the students rushed out of the classroom.
3. sharp / can / your mind / keep / A balanced diet（句子重組）
＝ A balanced diet can keep your mind sharp.
4. found / The students / their classroom door / when they arrived / locked（句子重組）
＝ The students found their classroom door locked when they arrived.
5. running / when you / Do not / brush your teeth / leave the water（句子重組）
＝ Do not leave the water running when you brush your teeth.
