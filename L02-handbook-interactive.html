Grammar Focus 1 分詞構句：簡化對等子句 ｜ S + V ..., V-ing / Vpp ...
範例 1 課文第 9 行
Toby and Helena did some research, finding that people make a subconscious judgment about a product within 90 seconds after they see it.
Toby 跟 Helena 做了一些研究，發現人們在看到一個產品後，會在九十秒內給出一個下意識的評價。
範例 2 課文第 31 行
Brands like Post-it and McDonald's have made this color a major part of their logos, trying to create an impression of fun and energy.
像是利貼或麥當勞等品牌，已經把這個顏色變成了他們商標的主色，試圖創造一種樂趣和活力的形象。
說明 1
當兩個由 and 相連的對等子句符合：① 主詞相同 ② 幾乎同時發生或有前後關係 時，就可以簡化成分詞構句。
The passengers got on the train , and they tried to find their seats.
乘客們登上火車，並試著尋找自己的座位。
說明 2（簡化步驟）
① 省略連接詞和第二個子句的主詞；② 將第二個子句的動詞改為現在分詞 V-ing（表示主動）或過去分詞 Vpp（表示被動）。
The passengers got on the train, and they tried to find their seats.
→ The passengers got on the train, trying to find their seats.
乘客們登上火車，試著尋找自己的座位。

句型手冊 ｜ Grammar Focus 2：據說∕據信∕據報導……
Grammar Focus 2 It is said / believed / reported that S + V
範例 課文第 25 行
It is believed that blue symbolizes intelligence and trust.
據信藍色象徵了智慧與信任。
說明 1
這個句型用來表達「據說∕據信∕據報導……」，常見於新聞、報導或正式寫作中，用來轉述「不特定多人」的看法或傳聞，而不是說話者自己的意見。基本結構為 It is said / believed / reported (that) S + V，口語或非正式文體中 that 可省略。
It is said (that) the new teacher is a Harvard graduate.
據說那位新老師是哈佛畢業生。
說明 2：虛主詞 It
句首的 It 是「虛主詞」，真正的主詞是後面的 that 子句。常搭配的動詞有 said（據說）、believed（據信）、reported（據報導）、thought（據認為）、rumored（據傳聞）等。
It is rumored (that) the big house by the river is haunted.
傳聞那間河邊的大房子鬧鬼。

Grammar Focus 3 in order to V ｜ 為了……（表目的∕原因）
範例 課文第 6 行
In order to find the best color for the logo, they decided to look into the use of colors in marketing psychology.
為了替商標找到最好的顏色，他們決定調查顏色在行銷心理學的運用。
說明 1
in order to V 表示目的或原因，相當於「為了……」，後面接原形動詞。口語中通常用 to V 即可，但 in order to 語氣更明確，可置於句首或句中。否定為 in order not to「為了不……」，不可簡化為 not to，也不能置於句首。
We study hard (in order) to pass the final exam.
= (In order) To pass the final exam, we study hard.
我們努力讀書為了通過期末考試。
We study hard in order not to fail the final exam.
我們努力讀書是為了不要期末考試不及格。

Grammar Focus 4 so + adj. / adv. + that S + V ｜ 如此……以致於……
範例 課文第 14 行
This shows color is so important that every logo needs the right one.
這顯示了顏色是如此重要，以致於每個商標都需要一個適合的顏色。
說明 1
so + adj. / adv. + that S + V 表示「如此……以致於……」，描述某個動作或狀態的程度大到引發後面的結果。so 後接形容詞或副詞，視前方動詞的種類而定。
The novel was so boring that I could not finish it.
這本小說太無聊，以致於我無法看完。
The thief ran so fast that the police could not catch him.
那賊跑得如此之快，以致於警方追不上他。

說明 2：相似的句型 such ... that ...
同樣表達「如此……以致於……」，但 such 後接名詞，或形容詞＋名詞。兩個句型間有時可以代換。
It was so hot that we all stayed indoors.
= It was such a hot day that we all stayed indoors.
天氣如此熱，以致於我們都待在室內。

句型手冊 ｜ 實力測驗：二、填充 ＆ 三、句型練習
🏆 二、填充 依中文完成英文句子
1. 據說建造這些埃及金字塔的人有可能是外星人。
It is said that... may have been...（完成句子）
It is said that the builders of these Egyptian pyramids may have been aliens.
2. 那名學生一直在打工，為了能賺到足夠的錢來應付大學支出。【統 108 年】
The student has been working part time in order to earn sufficient money to cover his college expenses.
🏆 三、句型練習 句子重組、改寫、合併
1.（句子重組）
at the Olympics, / He won / making his country / a gold medal / proud of him
He won a gold medal at the Olympics, making his country proud of him.
2.（句子重組）
the new weight-loss drug / will solve / It is / the problem of being overweight / reported that
It is reported that the new weight-loss drug will solve the problem of being overweight.
3.（句子重組）
attract customers, / are said to bring joy / their logo / uses colors that / In order to
In order to attract customers, their logo uses colors that are said to bring joy.
4.（句子改寫）Red is a color of celebration in Taiwanese culture, and it is never used in funerals（葬禮）.（用分詞構句 V-ing 或 Vpp 改寫）
Red is a color of celebration in Taiwanese culture, never used in funerals.
5.（句子合併）The light blue color looks peaceful. / It reminds me of the ocean or sky.（用 so ... that 合併）
The light blue color looks so peaceful that it reminds me of the ocean or sky.
