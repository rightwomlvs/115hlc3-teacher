第 2 題 (單選) According to the talk, what should you do FIRST when an earthquake strikes?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Speaker: "When an earthquake happens, you should take immediate action to ensure your safety. Firstly, drop down to your hands and knees."
中譯：地震發生時，應立即採取行動確保自身安全。首先，手與膝著地趴下。
解析：依聽力時序關鍵字 "Firstly"（首先），可知首要動作為 (B) Drop down to your hands and knees（趴下，手與膝著地）。
第 3 題 (單選) Why does the speaker suggest dropping down to your hands and knees?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Speaker: "This position provides stability and allows you to crawl to a safer location."
中譯：這個姿勢可以保持身體穩定，並能爬到較安全的地方。
解析：聽力講述趴下姿勢能提供 stability（穩定性）並能 crawl（爬行），故選 (C)。
第 4 題 (單選) When should you grab your bug-out bag?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Speaker: "Next, hold on to the furniture until the shaking stops. After that, if it is necessary to leave the building, grab your bug-out bag before moving to an open area."
中譯：接著，抓住家具直到搖晃停止。之後，如果有必要離開建築物，請在前往空曠區域前帶上你的防災包。
解析：搖晃停止且確認需要撤離建築物時，才拿取防災包 (bug-out bag)，故選 (C)。

二、會話與口說情境測驗：地震來襲 (When an Earthquake Happens)

仔細看對話漫畫並聽音檔作答
🔍 點選圖片可放大檢視課本對話漫畫 (Peter & Christy)
第 1 題 (單選) Where are Peter and Christy when the earthquake strikes?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Narration: "Peter and Christy are in their classroom, and an earthquake happens."
中譯：Peter 和 Christy 正在教室裡，地震來襲。故選 (B)。
第 2 題 (單選) What does Christy say when Peter is terrified?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Christy: "Take a deep breath. It's just an earthquake... Calm down. Follow the SOP. Stay under your desk until it's over."
中譯：深呼吸。不過就是地震而已……冷靜。照著標準程序走。躲到桌子下面直到地震停止。
第 3 題 (單選) What does Christy mean when she says "Hold your horses"?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Peter: "It stopped. Should we run?"
Christy: "Hold your horses. Use your bag to cover your head. We should go outside to the sports field, but there's no need to run."
中譯："Hold your horses" 為常用口語慣用語，意思是「稍安勿躁、且慢、不要急著跑」，故選 (A)。
第 4 題 (課本 Speaking Practice 1, p.28)
Scott: Mom, Toby invited me to his house. I'll be back before dinner.
Mom:          Did you finish your homework?
Scott: I did. Also, I cleaned my room and fed the dog. Can I go now?
Mom: That's great. Go ahead!
📌 【解答模式】試題解析
解析：媽媽要求兒子「等一下、別急著跑出門」，先確認功課做完了沒，應用 (C) Hold your horses.（稍安勿躁/且慢）。
第 5 題 (課本 Speaking Practice 2, p.28)
Olivia: Where are you going? Don't you know PE class was switched with Chinese class today?
Paula:          I totally forgot about that!
Olivia: I guess you didn't remember to bring your books, either.
Paula: What should I do?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析
解析：Paula 驚覺自己完全忘記調課這件事，表達震驚驚呼「我的天哪！」，依課本 Speaking Tips 應選 (C) Oh, my!。

三、因果關係聽力測驗：房間色彩與心理 (Cause and Effect)

仔細聽對話中的因果訊號詞作答
💡 聽力關鍵策略：Listening for Cause and Effect (因果訊號詞)

聽力中的「路標」詞彙能暗示接下來要表達的是原因還是結果：

原因 (Cause) + N/V-ing：because of / due to / as a result of / thanks to 原因 (Cause) + S+V：because / since / that's because 結果 (Effect) + S+V：therefore / as a result / thus / so / that's why
🔍 點選圖片可放大檢視課本因果訊號詞圖表 (p.49)
第 1 題 (課本原題 1) Why does the man like a white room?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Man: "That's interesting. I prefer white, since it can make a room look bigger."
中譯：真有意思。我比較喜歡白色，因為白色能讓室內空間看起來比較大。
解析：男士使用原因連接詞 "since"（因為）引出理由 "it can make a room look bigger"，故選 (D)。
第 2 題 (課本原題 2) What color do the speakers decide to use for their living room in the end?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Woman: "Green does feel fresh, but it doesn't match the color of our sofas. So, let's go with blue for the living room walls."
Man: "All right. You're the boss."
中譯：綠色確實感覺很清新，但跟我們沙發的顏色不搭。所以，客廳牆壁我們還是選藍色吧。 / 男：好吧，妳說了算。
解析：女士用結果訊號詞 "So" 做出最後決定選藍色，男士表示同意，故選 (B)。
第 3 題 (進階因果題) Why does the woman NOT want to paint the walls white?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Woman: "White gets dirty easily. That's why I've never used it."
中譯：白色很容易髒耶。這就是為什麼我從來沒用過白色。故選 (B)。
第 4 題 (進階因果題) Why do the speakers decide against choosing green?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Woman: "Green does feel fresh, but it doesn't match the color of our sofas."
中譯：綠色確實很清新，但跟我們沙發的顏色不搭。故選 (A)。

四、生活會話測驗：計程車的顏色 (The Colors of Taxis)

仔細看對話漫畫並聽音檔作答
🔍 點選圖片可放大檢視課本對話漫畫 (Leon & Eunice)
第 1 題 (單選) Why are regular taxis in Taiwan painted yellow?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Eunice: "Most of them. Regular taxis are painted yellow. It's probably because yellow is very easy to see from a distance."
中譯：大部分是。一般計程車漆成黃色。這可能是因為黃色從遠處看非常顯眼。故選 (B)。
第 2 題 (單選) Why were London cabs traditionally painted black?
📌 【解答模式】試題解析與原文對照
Leon: "Black paint was the cheapest option for taxis after World War II. Over time, the black taxi has become a cultural symbol of London."
中譯：二戰後黑色油漆是計程車最便宜的選擇。久而久之，黑色計程車成了倫敦的文化象徵。故選 (A)。
第 3 題 (課本 Speaking Practice 1, p.52)
Leon: We've been waiting for the taxi for 15 minutes. What's taking so long?
Eunice: It's the morning rush hour now.
Leon:         
Eunice: Yeah, so take it easy.
📌 【解答模式】試題解析
解析：Eunice 解釋是因為早上尖峰時段塞車，Leon 認同對方的解釋「有道理、原來如此」，依課本 Speaking Tips 應選 (A) That makes sense.。
第 4 題 (課本 Speaking Practice 2, p.52)
Ruby:          Why do so many salespeople wear blue or white shirts?
Bob: I think these colors help make them look professional.
Ruby: I see.
📌 【解答模式】試題解析
解析：Ruby 想表達自己很好奇並禮貌發問，應用 (C) I'm curious.（我很好奇 / 請教一下）作為開場白。
