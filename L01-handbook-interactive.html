說明 2：補語的類型不同，作用也不同
補語類型 作用 例句
形容詞
(adj.)		 描述狀態 May always keeps her room tidy.
S V O OC（adj.）｜ May 總是保持房間整齊。
現在分詞
(V-ing)		 表示主動動作 James left the music playing when he went out.
S V O OC（V-ing）｜ James 出門時讓音樂一直播放。
過去分詞
(Vpp)		 表示被動動作 She found her mirror broken when she got home.
S V O OC（Vpp）｜ 當她回到家時發現鏡子被打破了。

Grammar Focus 2 not only A but (also) B ｜ 不只 A 而且 B（對等連接詞）
範例 1 課文第 9 行
It is basically a survival kit not only for natural disasters but also for other emergencies, such as house fires.
它基本上是一個生存包，不只作為應對天然災難，還能用在其他緊急狀況，例如居家火災。
範例 2 課文第 27 行
You should not only keep the items updated but also make sure that you can grab your bag quickly.
你不只要持續更新內容物，還要確保能快速抓取你的包包。
說明 1：對等連接詞，強調兩個相關且同時存在的資訊
not only A but (also) B 用來強調兩個相關並同時存在的資訊，A 與 B 的語法結構需一致（同詞性或時態、同為片語或子句）。
My sister plays not only the piano but (also) the violin.
A（名詞） B（名詞）｜ 我姊姊不僅會彈鋼琴，還會拉小提琴。
My sister not only plays the piano but (also) plays the violin.
A（動詞片語） B（動詞片語）
It was not only cold but (also) windy yesterday.
A（形容詞） B（形容詞）｜ 昨天不只寒冷，還風大。
說明 2：A＋B 連接兩個名詞作為主詞時，動詞要與 B（第二個主詞）一致
Not only the players but (also) the coach was upset after the loss.
A（players） B（coach） V（與 B 一致）｜ 不僅球員們，還有教練在輸球後也感到悶悶不樂。

說明 3：用 Not only 開頭強調語氣時，後面子句要用倒裝
ordinary The team not only won the game but (also) set a new record.
inversion Not only did the team win the game, but they (also) set a new record.
倒裝（助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞）變成子句 but + S (+ also) + V ｜ 那支隊伍不僅贏得比賽，還創下新紀錄。

Grammar Focus 3 as soon as ｜ 一……就……（表時間順序）
範例 課文第 7 行
As soon as the shaking stopped, I reached for my B.O.B.
震動一停止，我就伸手去拿我的生存包。
說明 1：從屬連接詞，強調一件事緊接著另一件事發生
as soon as 連接兩個動作或事件子句，可放在句首或句中；as soon as 後接的是先發生的事件或動作。也可用 the moment / the minute 代替。
As soon as the meeting ended, the CEO left the meeting room.
= The CEO left the meeting room as soon as the meeting ended.
= The CEO left the meeting room the moment the meeting ended.
A（先發生）→ B（後發生）｜ 會議一結束，執行長就離開了會議室。

Grammar Focus 4 either A or B ｜ 不是 A 就是 B（二擇一）
範例 課文第 32 行
We can either be safe or be sorry.
我們不是安全度過，就是事後懊悔。
說明 1：表示「二擇一」的對等連接詞
either A or B 表示「二擇一」，連接兩個動作、事件或人，被連接的兩個部分需性質一致。
The keys are either on the table or in the bag.
A（介系詞片語） B（介系詞片語）｜ 鑰匙不是在桌上，就是在包包裡。
We can either go hiking or stay home this coming weekend.
A（動詞片語） B（動詞片語）｜ 我們接下來這週末可以去爬山或待在家裡。

🏆 二、填充 依中文完成英文句子
1. 地球的引力讓月亮保持圍繞著它移動。（keep + O + 現在分詞）
Earth's gravity keeps the moon moving around it.
2. 假以時日，蘭花不僅能裝飾居家，還能豐富臺灣的森林生態系統。【統 114 年】
Given time, orchids may not only decorate homes but also enrich Taiwan's forest ecosystem.
🏆 三、句型練習 句子重組、改寫、合併
句子重組either too expensive / these ingredients（食材）/ The chef found / or / not fresh enough
The chef found these ingredients either too expensive or not fresh enough.
句子重組started to shake, / the building / the students / hid under their desks / As soon as
As soon as the building started to shake, the students hid under their desks.
句子重組keeps Max / but also his part-time job / Not only / busy / his schoolwork
Not only his schoolwork but also his part-time job keeps Max busy.
句子改寫The moment Julian saw the car accident, he called the police.（用 As soon as ... 改寫）
As soon as Julian saw the car accident, he called the police.
句子合併Employees of this company can work in the office. Employees of this company can also work from home.（用 either ... or ... 合併）
Employees of this company can work either in the office or from home.
